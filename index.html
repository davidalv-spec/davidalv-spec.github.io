Idols

Idols: brand, store and content system

Idols sells authenticated sports and music memorabilia in Costa Rica (idolscr.com, @idols.cr). I co-own it (50%). I built the identity, the 47-page brand manual, the Shopify store, and a repeatable system for producing Instagram content.

Visit the store Instagram @idols.cr

Brand system

An Obsidian base with a Champagne accent. Playfair Display for headlines, Montserrat for body and interface. The manual sets rules so any piece can be checked: the diamond seal appears once per piece, a dark scrim sits behind text on photos, and nothing is placed over a signature, crest, hologram or number, because those are the proof of authenticity.

The manual also defines seven formats (A to G), an editorial calendar with a 44-story cycle, and an audit of existing work that found four competing styles and ranked ten fixes by impact. I retired one piece that used realistic player portraits because of image-rights risk and the brand's authenticity promise.
